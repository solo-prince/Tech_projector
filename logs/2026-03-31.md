## 🚀 Project Update - AgroSense v2.0 Field Node Optimization

**Date:** 2026-03-31

**Progress:**
Completed initial integration and calibration runs for the new environmental sensors on the ESP32 platform. The focus for this sprint was on optimizing power consumption for long-term remote deployment (battery-powered nodes) and validating data accuracy under simulated field conditions.

**Technical Work:**
- **Sensor Integration:** Successfully integrated the DHT22 (temperature/humidity) and a capacitive soil moisture sensor (v1.2) with the ESP32-WROOM-32 microcontroller.
- **Low Power Logic:** Implemented a Deep Sleep cycle using `esp_sleep_enable_timer_wakeup()`. The node wakes every 15 minutes, performs sensor readings, publishes data via MQTT over Wi-Fi, and then re-enters Deep Sleep. The active cycle duration (read/transmit) was reduced to approximately 4 seconds to minimize power draw.
- **Calibration:** Developed a two-point calibration routine for the soil moisture sensor, defining dry (air) and saturated (water) values to map the analog readings (0-4095) to a percentage scale (0-100%).

**Issues & Fixes:**
- **Issue:** Experienced significant data drift and instability from the capacitive soil moisture sensor during initial continuous testing. The readings fluctuated wildly after a few hours, even when the moisture level remained constant.
- **Fix:** Debugging indicated that the sensor was susceptible to voltage fluctuations during the ESP32's Wi-Fi initialization phase. The fix involved adding a stabilization delay (100ms) after powering up the sensor and before taking the analog reading. Additionally, ensuring the sensor's VCC rail was isolated during the Wi-Fi transmission phase improved stability.

**Next Steps:**
- **Power Consumption Analysis:** Finalize power consumption measurements (mA draw during sleep and active states) to accurately estimate battery life for various configurations.
- **Backend Integration:** Begin development of the data ingestion pipeline using InfluxDB and Grafana to visualize the sensor data collected from the MQTT broker.
- **Enclosure Design:** Design and 3D print weather-resistant enclosures for initial field deployment testing.
